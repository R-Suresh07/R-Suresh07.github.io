New preprint: [Proper Scoring Rules for Agentic Uncertainty Quantification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24756). We introduce the Trajectory Proper Score (TPS), a predictor-agnostic family of strictly proper, trajectory-level scoring rules for evaluating uncertainty in LLM agents.
